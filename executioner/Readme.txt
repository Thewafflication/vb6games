Users Manual - Executioner by Corey McInroy (Creptic)

=================
Introduction
=================
              
	Through your journey your faithful companion has been captured by the executioner . You travel through the dungeon with success and reach the lair of the executioner. While entering the lair you find yourself captured as well. With a smile the executioner approaches, stinking of flesh he opens his mouth and speaks.

 “ You are a worthy adventurer to have made it this far” he pauses and scratches his head and turns to leave. But hesitates
 “ I myself have honor, therefore I will give you one last chance” he leans his head closer.
 “Answer my phrase and I shall let you and your companion go free, if you fail You both shall die a horrible death!”
 
=================
Playing the game
=================
-All options can be accessed by the menu bar in the top left of the game screen.
-To start a game click on game then choose 1 or 2 players.  
-A check mark indicates how many players are chosen. )
-To change the amount of time and hints click on the options then Advanced Options.

=================
Using the Game screen
=================        
-Use your mouse to choose a letter, If a correct letter is chosen it will be displayed  in the phrase, 
 If a wrong answer is chosen then bad things happen to your friend.

-Click on the hint button for a letter, if it disappears all your hints are used up  (You can change the amount of hints in advanced options). 
	

-When/if you want to solve without guessing all the letters manually with the mouse, click on the solve button and type in the phrase (not case sensitive) then press enter on your keyboard. If nothing happens then you have not typed the correct phrase in.   

	
	

**After each game hit F1 to start a new game or click on the menu bar options and start a new game or click on the animated skeleton in the main window.


=================
Installing
=================

-To install executioner to your HardDrive click on the install progran, After installed, run the game from the file called Executioner.exe in the folder you installed to, or use the shortcut in the start menu or Desktop.

=================
System Requirements
=================

You will need the Following to play executioner:

Windows 95/98 or better
Resolution of 800X600 or better
Over 16 colours
Mouse
Keyboard 
2 Megs of Hard drive Space
Sound Card 
Sense of humor ( always helps )
